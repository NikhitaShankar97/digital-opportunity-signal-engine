# Sales discovery brief

## Client situation

A growth-equity research team screens consumer companies manually. Analysts copy digital indicators from different sources, apply inconsistent judgment, and cannot easily explain why one company was prioritized over another.

## Decision to improve

Which companies should enter deeper research first based on recent digital momentum, traffic scale, and engagement quality?

## Value hypothesis

A standardized Similarweb signal feed can shorten initial screening, make analyst decisions more consistent, and allow the firm to monitor a larger universe without replacing investment judgment.

## POC scope

- One peer group: Rhode, Rare Beauty, and Glossier
- One observation month: July 2026
- Publicly visible Similarweb estimates collected manually
- Configurable peer-relative scoring and plain-English explanations
- Analyst-facing Streamlit experience
- Production design for licensed Batch API or managed Data Feed delivery

## Discovery questions

1. How many companies and peer groups are screened each month?
2. What decision follows an initial digital screen?
3. Which digital behaviors have historically preceded a successful research target?
4. What history and refresh frequency are required?
5. Which countries and devices matter to each investment thesis?
6. What false-positive rate can analysts tolerate?
7. Where should signals appear: research portal, warehouse, CRM, or alerts?
8. Who owns metric definitions, access controls, and analyst feedback?
9. What would make the buyer expand from a POC to production?
