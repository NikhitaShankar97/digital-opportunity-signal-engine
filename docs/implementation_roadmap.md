# 30-day implementation roadmap

| Period | Focus | Deliverable | Primary owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-5 | Align | Decisions, peer groups, success measures, entitlements | Business sponsor + Data Strategist |
| Days 6-10 | Connect | Licensed delivery to agreed landing zone and identity mapping | Client data lead + Similarweb technical team |
| Days 11-17 | Prove | Quality rules, historical backfill, score calibration, analyst review | Data Strategist + analysts |
| Days 18-24 | Integrate | Curated table, research-product integration, access controls, monitoring | Client engineering lead |
| Days 25-30 | Adopt | Pilot training, feedback, operating runbook, expansion decision | Account Management + client product owner |

## Expansion roadmap

1. Add history and alerting for sustained momentum rather than one-month movement.
2. Expand peer groups and geography/device segmentation.
3. Add search, referral, app, and audience datasets where licensed and decision-relevant.
4. Integrate analyst feedback and downstream research outcomes for score calibration.
5. Operationalize usage, adoption, freshness, and business-impact reporting.
